# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-22
### Added
- A seaplane: a six-seat bush plane on floats that takes off from and lands on water (and on the ground). It floats at the surface, and comes back up if pushed under.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-22
### Added
- Seven aircraft you can fly, on Bedrock and Java: a paraglider, a hot-air balloon, a glider, a light aircraft, a business jet, a gyrocopter and a helicopter. Each has its own model, spinning propellers and rotors, and tilts as it climbs and banks.
- One flight model for both editions: lift, stall, glide ratios, take-off runs, a winch launch for the glider, a hovering helicopter, and a balloon that holds its height and drifts with a wind that turns with height.
- Rising air over campfires, lava, and sand in the daytime.
- Bail out in the air with sneak; carry a parachute and it opens after a moment, or when you jump. Steer it, and it is packed away when you land.
- Hunting from the hot-air balloon with the Realistic Hunting add-on: anchor it, shoot through the basket, and sneak zooms the scope instead of getting you out.
- Hard landings and crashes damage the aircraft; pilotless aircraft come down by themselves.
- Pilot's Handbook, recipes, English and Danish.
